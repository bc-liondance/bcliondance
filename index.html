About Us

Boston College Lion Dance was founded in 2025 by a group of driven college students seeking to collaborate with like-minded individuals who are passionate about spreading the Asian performance art of Lion Dance.

We serve to spread the traditional culture of Lion Dance to the BC and surrounding Boston/Newton community, with the goal of providing an inclusive and team-oriented environment through performing arts.
